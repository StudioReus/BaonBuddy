    Budgeting Guide for Students

    50/30/20 Rule

    Allocate 50% for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for savings.

    Weekly Allowance Plan

    Divide your weekly allowance into food, transportation, school needs, and emergency buffer.

    Save Before Spending

    Set aside a portion of your allowance first before buying non-essential items.

    Savings Tips

    • Keep a daily spending log.
    • Use cash envelopes for food and transportation.
    • Avoid buying items that are not part of your budget.
    • Set a weekly savings goal like ₱100 or ₱200.
    • Save extra money from your allowance for emergencies or school projects.
